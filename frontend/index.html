How Risk Is Decided

We look at the average of First_Term and Second_Term marks, past failures and absences. Rules are checked from the top: High first, then Medium, otherwise Low.

High risk

Needs help soon

  • Average marks below 10
  • or 2 or more past failures
Medium risk

Keep an eye

  • Average marks below 14
  • or more than 15 absences
Low risk

Doing fine

  • None of the above

Example: First_Term 8 and Second_Term 10 give an average of 9, so the risk is High. After you click Predict, the matching box lights up.
